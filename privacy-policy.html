Legal

Privacy Policy

This page explains how Lefuse handles information submitted through this website.

Information you choose to provide

When you use the contact form, Lefuse receives the details you submit, such as your name, email address, optional phone number, and message. We use that information to understand and respond to your request.

Contact-form processing

The contact form is processed by Formspree, a third-party form service. Information submitted through the form is transmitted to that service so the message can be delivered to Lefuse. Review Formspree’s policies before submitting sensitive information.

How Lefuse treats personal information

Lefuse does not sell personal information submitted through this website. We aim to limit the information requested to what is useful for responding to an inquiry.

Product-specific information

Lefuse products may have their own privacy notices when their features and data practices require additional detail. Those notices apply to the relevant product experience.

Questions

For privacy questions, contact Lefuse.
